    About Us

    Building money confidence, one young mind at a time.

    Finance For Movement is a youth-led organization committed to educating K–8 students across the world on essential personal finance skills. We raise awareness of harmful habits like overconsumption and empower future generations to make thoughtful and confident financial decisions.

    We specifically serve Title 1 school students, funded through community-driven art in regions nearby Gwinnett County, Georgia. Our resources are fully developed by teens and are focused for kids with fun, relatable content for their age.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Who are these resources for?

    Anyone. This resource is designed for schools, nonprofits, and any individuals or organizations serving students in grades K–8. The materials work for after-school programs and home learning, and all full-length resources are provided at no cost.

    Why use our resources?

    We are youth-led and understand student needs. Our resources are fully developed by teens and focused for kids with fun, relatable content for their age. All full-length resources are completely free.

    What do you get?

    Each month, you will receive 1–2 resources (slides + recorded). These can be used as teaching materials for English exposure, personal finance lessons, or both.

    How do you utilize these resources?

    The materials are flexible and adaptable. You may use them as a full lesson or a mini workshop, and customize them based on your group size and time constraints. Examples are available in our Resource Hub.
