    About Us

    Building money confidence, one young mind at a time.

    Finance For Movement is a youth-led organization committed to educating K–8 students across the world on essential personal finance skills. We raise awareness of harmful habits like overconsumption and empower future generations to make thoughtful and confident financial decisions.

    We specifically serve Title 1 school students, funded through community-driven art in regions nearby Gwinnett County, Georgia. Our resources are fully developed by teens and are focused for kids with fun, relatable content for their age.

    Our Mission

    To make financial literacy accessible, engaging, and fun for every young learner — starting with the communities that need it most. (Full mission statement coming soon.)

    Our Vision

    What We Do

    Free, teen-built financial education that meets students where they are.

    Hands-on Workshops

    Interactive in-person workshops teach fundamental concepts — like telling “needs” from “wants” — through art, crafts, and play that K–8 students actually enjoy.

    Monthly Resources

    Users receive 1–2 resources every month (slides + recorded lessons). Use them for English exposure, personal finance lessons, or both — fully free.

    Community Art Fundraising

    We fund education for Title 1 students through handcrafted, community-driven art — turning creativity into classroom impact.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Who are these programs for?

    Anyone. Our programs are designed for schools, nonprofits, and any individuals or organizations serving students in grades K–8. They work for after-school programs and home learning, and everything we offer is provided at no cost.

    Why work with us?

    We are youth-led and understand student needs. Our materials are fully developed by teens and focused for kids with fun, relatable content for their age. Everything we offer is completely free.

    What do you offer?

    Hands-on workshops, free literacy kits for organizations, and community events. Reach out and we'll help you find the right fit for your group.

    How do organizations receive a literacy kit?

    Visit our Literacy Kits page to see what's included and request a kit. Kits are geared toward 3rd–5th graders and we prioritize immigrant and/or low-income students and organizations.
