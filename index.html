    Why It Matters

    A financial literacy gap is leaving young people behind.

    According to the National Endowment for Financial Education:

    1 in 5

    U.S. teens lack basic personal finance skills.

    32%

    of teens cannot distinguish between a credit and a debit card.

    70%+

    of teens report lacking confidence in their personal finance knowledge.

    ~70%

    of youth want financial education but lack access to it.

    What We Do

    Free, teen-built financial education that meets students where they are.

    Hands-on Workshops

    Interactive in-person workshops teach fundamental concepts, like telling “needs” from “wants,” through art, crafts, and play that K–8 students actually enjoy.

    New for 26-27

    Literacy Kits

    Free kits for organizations serving 3rd to 5th graders. Each one includes a piggy bank, our money management workbook, a finance tip bookmark, and a handwritten note from our volunteers.

    Community Art Fundraising

    We fund education for Title 1 students through handcrafted, community-driven art that turns creativity into classroom impact.
