    About Us

    We are a youth-led 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization serving underrepresented elementary school students to foster personal finance skills through creativity.

    Who We Are

    Building money confidence, one young mind at a time.

    Finance For Movement is a youth-led nonprofit organization committed to bridging the gap in financial literacy skills among underserved students through hands-on workshops and literacy kits.

    Our Mission

    Our mission is to improve the understanding of financial literacy and provide hands-on education by providing financial literacy workshops and kits funded through community driven art.
